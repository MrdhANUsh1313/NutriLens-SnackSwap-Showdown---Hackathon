Find Exact Text Duplicates Without Spreadsheet Chaos

Duplicate Text Finder validates incoming text records, compares entries using strict exact matching, persists every change locally to synchronized records.csv and records.json files, and generates live analytics, Matplotlib charts, threshold warnings, and multi-format audit reports.

7-Step Demonstration Walkthrough

Ready to Verify
  1. Launch App: You are on the branded welcome screen with live CSV/JSON state.
  2. Add Entry: Open Add & Validate Entry to save a new text record.
  3. Test Validation: Click any invalid-input preset or submit blank/bad data to verify graceful error recovery.
  4. Detect Duplicates: Run Duplicate Detection to separate exact duplicates from unique entries.
  5. View Analytics: Inspect live summary tables, warnings, and Matplotlib charts.
  6. Verify Persistence: Restart Flask and confirm new records remain in data/records.csv & data/records.json.
  7. Export Report: Download or preview Markdown, Plain-Text, or CSV reports.
